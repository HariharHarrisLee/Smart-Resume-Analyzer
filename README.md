# Smart Resume Analyzer

**Smart Resume Analyzer** is an AI-powered web app designed to help job seekers optimize their resumes for specific job postings and improve their chances of getting noticed by recruiters and ATS systems.

---

## **Problem**
Many students and job seeking candidates struggle to make their resumes to individual job descriptions.  
- Resumes often miss critical keywords, reducing ATS compatibility (Applicant Tracking System).  
- They may not clearly highlight their achievements or quantify impact.  
- Getting personalized, actionable feedback from experts is expensive and time-consuming.

---

## **Solution**
Smart Resume Analyzer provides instant, actionable feedback on resumes:  
- **Match Score & Keyword Analysis:** Compares your resume to the job description and identifies missing keywords.  
- **AI-Powered Feedback:** Generates a concise summary of strengths and areas for improvement.  
- **Rewritten Resume Bullets:** Suggests optimized, achievement-focused bullet points that naturally incorporate job-specific keywords.  
- **ATS Optimization Tips:** Offers advice to improve chances of passing automated resume screening systems.

All of this works in real-time through a **simple Streamlit interface**, making resume improvement quick and accessible.

---

## **Impact**
- Saves job seekers valuable time in customizing resumes for different job applications.  
- Increases visibility to recruiters and ATS systems by highlighting missing keywords.  
- Provides recruiter-level insights without hiring an expensive resume coach.
